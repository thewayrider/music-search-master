How to do it in Antigravity on your Lenovo:

When your laptop arrives and you launch Antigravity:

Method 1: The Antigravity UI (1-Click)
On the Antigravity Welcome screen, click "Clone Git Repository..." (or press Ctrl + Shift + P and type Git: Clone).
Paste your repository URL: https://github.com/thewayrider/bookmark-sync.git
Pick your local projects folder on the laptop.
Antigravity will clone it and pop up a prompt: "Would you like to open the cloned repository?" → Click Open.
Method 2: The Integrated Terminal
Open Antigravity and press Ctrl + ` (backtick) to open the terminal.
Run:
bash
git clone https://github.com/thewayrider/bookmark-sync.git
Click File → Open Folder and select bookmark-sync.

-----

Loading into Comet/Browser on the Lenovo:

Once cloned:

In Comet (or Chrome) on the Lenovo, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
Click Load unpacked.
Point it to your newly cloned bookmark-sync/extension folder.
Click the icon, save your URL and key once, and you are immediately running with the exact same 1-click button!
